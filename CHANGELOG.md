# Changelog

## LCD version (v3.x) - moved

The LCD version now has its own repository, with its history in its changelog:
[Pico-Latency-Tester-LCD](https://github.com/Casio-Byte/Pico-Latency-Tester-LCD).

## v2.1 - PC app

Windows app (Dear ImGui + D3D11) for a Pico / Pico W with the sensor and the
onboard LED: built-in full-screen or windowed target (F11 to toggle), latency
breakdown via clock sync, scope, history, firmware installer.

---

The PC app version has been tested and confirmed working on real hardware.
